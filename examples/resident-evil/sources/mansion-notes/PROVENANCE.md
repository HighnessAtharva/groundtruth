# Where these records come from

Every file in this folder was written by hand by the author of the article, from
notes taken during a playthrough in February 2026. Nothing here was copied from a
wiki, a strategy guide, or any other published source. Titles, names and dates are
facts and are not copyrightable expression. No third-party prose ships in this
repository.

Two records carry `synthetic: true`. Those numbers are invented, and they are
invented on purpose so the example can demonstrate a claim going wrong:

- `items/armor-key.md` says the Armor Key opens four doors. The article says
  three. That disagreement is what produces the CONTRADICTED verdict.
- `rooms/dining-hall.md` carries an invented item count.

Treat this folder as a worked example of a source you wrote yourself. That is the
honest answer to "where do I get sources for a piece about a thing that has no
API", and it is how most real grounding folders get built.
